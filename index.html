BridgePass

A skills passport that turns evidence into employability, even when paperwork is missing.

Demo only: scores use self-reported data and simple rules. A live version needs partner assessors, and legal steps must be confirmed with the host country's authorities and UNHCR/ILO partners.

The problem

Skilled refugees lose years to "brain waste" because three barriers stack up: their qualifications cannot be verified, they cannot legally or financially get paid, and they lack local references and networks. Fixing one barrier alone leaves the others in place.

The solution

BridgePass is one passport with three layers. Verify: evidence-based competency scoring (documents, references, work samples, task challenges) in place of paper-only checks. Unlock: a personal readiness checklist for work authorization, ID and payment access, including remote-work and mobile-money routes. Connect: matching to returnships, fellowships and mentors with hiring partners, based on verified skills.

Roadmap

  • Pilot (0-3 months): 3 occupations, 50 candidates, 5 hiring partners.
  • Validate: compare passport scores against assessor interviews and employer outcomes.
  • Scale: add regulators' recognition pathways and an assessor network; export a shareable, candidate-owned passport.

Risks: self-reported data can be inflated (mitigated by reference checks and live challenges); rules differ by country (mitigated by partner-maintained rule sets); privacy of displaced people (candidate-owned data, minimal sharing).
